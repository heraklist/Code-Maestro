## Capability Freeze

Following Pass 5 acceptance, breadth-oriented taxonomy expansion is frozen for the first-generation architecture.

A new top-level capability family may be proposed only when real task/eval evidence shows that:

1. a recurring responsibility is not cleanly representable by the current families/shared intelligence;
2. the gap causes routing, correctness, safety, evidence, or context-cost problems;
3. the proposed boundary improves composition rather than duplicating an existing concern.

Capability Freeze is therefore an **eval-reopenable governance rule**, not a claim of universal permanent completeness.

## Relationship to later design amendments

Later approved design amendments, including Command-Gated Self-Evolution and repository logging governance, do not reopen Pass 5 breadth research by themselves. They are governance/context/evidence mechanisms composed from the accepted architecture unless future eval evidence proves otherwise.
